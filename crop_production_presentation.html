💡 Key Insights

🏆 Top Producer

Kerala dominates with 126+ Billion tonnes, driven by Coconut production (counted in nuts, not weight).

🌾 Crop Diversity

124 unique crops across 33 states. Rice, Wheat, and Sugarcane form the backbone of Indian agriculture.

📅 Seasonal Patterns

Whole Year crops contribute highest production. Kharif (monsoon) shows more variability than Rabi (winter).

📊 Key Correlation

Area-Production correlation (+0.62) is strong, but Year shows no significant trend — yield improvement is needed.

🎯 Conclusion

This analysis of 246K+ records (1997-2015) reveals that Indian agriculture is regionally concentrated, with Southern states leading. While production correlates with area, year-over-year yield hasn't improved significantly — suggesting focus should shift from land expansion to productivity enhancement through modern farming techniques.
